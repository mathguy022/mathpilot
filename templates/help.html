Help & Accessibility

Keyboard shortcuts, mathematical input guidelines, and accessibility features in MathPilot.

⌨️ Mathematical Keyboard Shortcuts

You can use your physical keyboard or the on-screen math palette to type expressions:

Symbol / ActionKeyboard ShortcutExample Input
Fraction/ or \frac1/3 → $\frac{1}{3}$
Power / Exponent^(3x+1)^2 → $(3x+1)^2$
Square Root\sqrt\sqrt{x} → $\sqrt{x}$
Derivative notationf'(x) or df/dxf'(x) → $f'(x)$
Greek letter $\pi$pi or \pi2*pi*r → $2\pi r$
Subscript variable_u_1 → $u_1$

♿ Accessibility & Screen Readers

MathPilot uses semantic HTML and standard ARIA attributes for seamless navigation with assistive tech:

  • ARIA Math Markup: All mathematical formulas are rendered with accessible KaTeX and MathLive spoken speech text.
  • Tab Navigation: Move through problem steps, hints, buttons, and formula inputs using Tab and Shift + Tab.
  • High Contrast & Fonts: Designed with accessible WCAG AA contrast standards, clear mathematical serif/sans-serif fonts, and visible keyboard-focus indicators.
  • Keyboard Submission: Press Enter within the active math field to check your step immediately.
