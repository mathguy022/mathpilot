Complete Verified Solution

Instructor-approved solution for $f(x) = (3x + 1)^2$:

Step 1: Identify the inner function.
Let $u = 3x + 1$. The outer function is $f(u) = u^2$.

Step 2: Apply the chain rule.
$$\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$$
$$f'(x) = 2(3x + 1) \cdot \frac{d}{dx}[3x + 1] = 2(3x + 1)(3)$$

Step 3: Simplify constants.
$$f'(x) = 6(3x + 1) = 18x + 6$$

Instructor: Dr. Lee · Department of Mathematics

Section 2.5: The Chain Rule

The Chain Rule states that for a composite function $F(x) = f(g(x))$:

$$F'(x) = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$$

Power Chain Rule Special Case:

If $F(x) = (g(x))^n$, then:

$$F'(x) = n(g(x))^{n-1} \cdot g'(x)$$

Tip: Always identify the inner expression $u = g(x)$ first. Differentiate the outer function, keeping the inside unchanged, and then multiply by the derivative of the inside.
