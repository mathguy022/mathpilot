Chain Rule Reference Guide

Dr. Lee

Fundamental Theorem of the Chain Rule:

$$\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$$

Common Pitfall: Students often compute $f'(g(x))$ but forget to multiply by the derivative of the inner function $g'(x)$. In polynomial powers $(ax+b)^n$, the derivative is $n(ax+b)^{n-1} \cdot a$.

Related Rates Geometry & Formulas

Dr. Sarah Wilson

Cone Volume & Time Derivatives:

$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h \implies \frac{dV}{dt} = 2\pi r^2 \frac{dr}{dt} \quad (\text{if } h=2r)$$

Strategy: Express the volume formula in a single variable before taking the derivative with respect to time $t$. Remember to use the chain rule on $r^3$: $\frac{d}{dt}[r^3] = 3r^2 \frac{dr}{dt}$.
